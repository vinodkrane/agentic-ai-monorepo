# Annual Leave Policy

Applies to: all permanent staff. Owner: People Team. Last reviewed: March 2026.

## Entitlement

Full time staff get 25 days annual leave per year plus bank holidays. After five
years of continuous service this goes up to 27 days. Part time staff get a pro rata
amount based on contracted hours.

The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

## Carrying over leave

You can carry over up to 5 unused days into the next leave year. Carried over days
must be used by 30 June, after that they are lost. Anything above 5 days needs
written approval from your line manager and the People Team, and is only approved
in exceptional cases such as long term sickness or parental leave.

## Booking leave

Book leave in the HR system at least two weeks in advance for anything longer than
three days. Your line manager should approve or decline within five working days.
During the December change freeze, teams on the on-call rota need to agree cover
before leave is approved.

## Buying and selling leave

Once a year, in the February window, you can buy up to 5 extra days. The cost is
taken from salary over the following 12 months. Selling leave is not allowed.
