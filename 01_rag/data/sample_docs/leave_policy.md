# Annual Leave Policy

## How much leave you get

Full-time staff get 25 days of paid annual leave each year. This is on top of public holidays.

Part-time staff get leave based on the days they work. For example, someone who works 3 days a week gets 15 days of leave.

After 5 years of service, you get 2 extra days of leave each year.

## Booking leave

Book leave through the HR portal at least 2 weeks before you want to take it. Your manager must approve it.

For leave longer than 10 working days in a row, ask your manager at least 6 weeks before.

## Carrying leave over

You can carry up to 5 unused days into the next leave year. Carried days must be used by 31 March, or they are lost.

If you could not take leave because of long-term sickness, speak to HR. Different rules apply.

## Sick leave

If you are sick, tell your manager before 10am on your first day off. If you are off for more than 7 days in a row, you need a fit note from a doctor.
