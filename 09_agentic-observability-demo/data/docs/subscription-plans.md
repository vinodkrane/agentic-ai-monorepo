# Subscription plans

StreamBox has three plans.

Basic costs £5.99 per month. You can watch on one screen at a time in HD.

Standard costs £9.99 per month. You can watch on two screens at a time in Full HD and download shows on two phones or tablets.

Premium costs £14.99 per month. You can watch on four screens at a time in 4K Ultra HD with spatial audio.

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Price changes start from your next billing date.
