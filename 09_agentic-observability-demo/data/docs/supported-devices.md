# Supported devices

You can watch StreamBox on smart TVs made from 2019 onwards, Android phones and tablets running Android 10 or later, iPhones and iPads running iOS 16 or later, and web browsers such as Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari.

Games consoles are not supported yet.

You can sign in on up to 6 devices on one account. To remove an old device, go to Account > Devices.
