# Travel and Expenses Policy

Applies to: all staff. Owner: Finance.

## Claiming

Submit claims through the expenses system within 60 days of spending the money.
Claims older than 60 days are rejected unless Finance agrees in advance. Every claim
needs a receipt, a photo from your phone is fine.

## Travel

Standard class rail is the default. First class is only allowed if it is cheaper
than standard on the day, and you need to attach evidence of both prices.
Flights within the UK need approval from a head of department.

Mileage in your own car is paid at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles in the
tax year and 25p per mile after that.

## Hotels and meals

Hotel limits are 150 pounds per night in London and 110 pounds per night elsewhere
in the UK, including breakfast. When staying away overnight you can claim up to
30 pounds per day for evening meals. Alcohol cannot be claimed.

## Equipment

Home office equipment over 100 pounds must be ordered through the IT catalogue, not
claimed as an expense.
